        How to play Scoundrel

        Fight your way through a 44-card dungeon. Clear every card to win. If your health hits 0, you lose.

        The dungeon

        • ♠ ♣ Monsters 2–10, J=11, Q=12, K=13, A=14 (26 cards)
        • ♦ Weapons 2–10 (9 cards)
        • ♥ Potions 2–10 (9 cards)

        You start with 20 health, which is also the maximum.

        Each turn

        Cards are drawn until a room of 4 is showing. Then choose:

        • Avoid: all 4 cards go to the bottom of the deck in order. You cannot avoid two rooms in a row.
        • Face: resolve 3 of the 4 cards in any order. The last card stays and becomes the first card of the next room.

        Resolving cards

        • Weapon ♦: equip it immediately. Your old weapon and every monster stacked on it are discarded.
        • Potion ♥: heal by its value (max 20). Only one potion per room heals. Any extra potions are discarded with no effect.
        • Monster ♠♣: bare-handed, you take its full value. With a weapon, you take monster − weapon (minimum 0), and the monster is stacked on the weapon.

        The weapon rule

        Once a weapon has slain a monster, it can only be used on monsters of equal or lower value than its last kill.

        Example: equip ♦5

        1. Fight ♠J (11) and take 11 − 5 = 6. Last kill: 11.
        2. Fight ♣9 (9 ≤ 11 ✔) and take 4. Last kill: 9.
        3. Fight ♠9 (9 ≤ 9 ✔, equal is fine) and take 4.
        4. ♠10 (10 > 9 ✘): the weapon can't be used, so you fight bare-handed for 10.

        Equipping a new weapon resets this history.

        If your weapon can be used, you may still choose to fight bare-handed. That keeps the weapon's last-kill value high for a bigger monster later.

        The potion rule

        Example: at 12 health, a room holds ♥6 and ♥4.

        1. Drink ♥6 and go to 18.
        2. Drink ♥4 in the same room: it is discarded with no effect, and you stay at 18.

        End of the game

        • Win: resolve every card. Your score is your remaining health.
        • Lose: your health reaches 0. Your score is minus the total value of the monsters left in the dungeon.
        • When the deck runs out, the final room must be resolved completely.

        Keyboard

        • 1–4: resolve the card in that slot (uses your weapon when possible)
        • Shift+1–4: fight that monster bare-handed
        • A avoid · N new game · R restart · ? rules · Esc close
        • Tab moves between cards and buttons. Enter or Space activates them.
